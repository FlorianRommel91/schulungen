Part 1

Why information security matters at GeoIntel

Our clients hand us data that decides how offshore wind farms and infrastructure get built. Losing it, leaking it or delivering wrong numbers is not an IT problem, it is a business problem.

🔒
Confidentiality

Ground investigation data, tender documents, method statements and client contracts are confidential by default. Only people who need them for their project get access.

✅
Integrity

Data must be accurate, complete and traceable. That is why work stays in LabHarmony and SharePoint, with version history, instead of on local drives or in chat attachments.

☁️
Availability

Everything lives in the cloud. If your account is compromised or your laptop is lost, the data is still there, but so is the risk of someone else getting in.

📜
Our promise

The IMS Policy (M01), signed by the Managing Directors, commits GeoIntel to protecting confidentiality, integrity and availability of all information entrusted to us. Certification proves it to clients.

💡
What this means for you: you do not need to know ISO 27001. You need to know the handful of rules in Part 2, apply them, and speak up when something looks wrong. Registers, audits and the technical side are handled by the Managing Directors, the Technical Lead and the IMS coordination.

Part 2

Your daily rules

Eight rules cover almost every situation. They come straight from our policies; the source is shown on each card.

One account, one person, strong password

Your account is yours. Never share it or your password, not even with a colleague on holiday. Passwords: at least 12 characters, upper and lower case, digits and special characters, never reused.

R03 Access Control & Password Policy
Lock your screen, clear your desk

Lock the laptop every time you walk away (Windows: Win + L). Auto-lock after 5 minutes at most. Nothing confidential stays on the desk or the screen when you leave.

R03 / R04
Look after your laptop

Never leave it unattended in public areas, vehicles or hotel rooms. Keep the operating system and antivirus up to date, encryption (BitLocker) switched on, and install updates when asked.

R04 Mobile Working Policy
Working in public: assume someone is watching

On trains, in hotels and at client sites use a privacy filter for sensitive data and make sure nobody can read your screen. Avoid printing; if paper is unavoidable, lock it away and destroy it afterwards.

R04 Mobile Working Policy
Private phone: cloud only

GeoIntel does not issue phones. If you use your own for Teams or email: screen lock on, no project files stored locally, access only through the approved cloud apps.

R04 Mobile Working Policy
Only what your project needs

Access is granted per project by the project manager or a Managing Director. Do not try to open data you were not given access to, and tell your project manager when you no longer need access.

R02 / R03
Company devices for company work

No unapproved software, no disabled security functions, no private use beyond the occasional exception. Do not store private data in GeoIntel systems.

R02 Acceptable Use of Assets Policy
AI tools: text only, never data

AI may help you polish an email or a paragraph. Never paste client data, geological or project data, LabHarmony content, source code or personal data into an AI tool. AI output is a draft you check, not a result you deliver.

R07 AI Usage Policy
📱
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is currently not enforced at GeoIntel; the Managing Directors have accepted this risk. That makes your password the only lock on the door, which is why rule 1 matters more here than in most companies.

Part 3 · Exercise

Allowed or not?

Decide for each situation. You get the answer immediately.

A colleague is on leave and you urgently need a file from his LabHarmony project. He sends you his login over Teams.
Shared accounts and passing on passwords are strictly prohibited (R03). Ask a Managing Director or the project manager to grant you access to the project instead.
You use an AI assistant to improve the English of an internal email that contains no project or client details.
That is exactly the permitted use in R07: text refinement without sensitive content. You still read and take responsibility for the result.
You paste a table of CPT results into an AI chat to have it summarised for a client report.
Project and geological data must never go into public AI tools, and AI is not used for technical deliverables at all (R07).
You work on the train and put a privacy filter on the screen before opening a tender document.
Correct behaviour under R04: sensitive information in public places only with a privacy filter and no one able to look over your shoulder.
You copy the project folder to a USB stick so you can keep working during a flight without internet.
Data belongs in the approved cloud environments so that it stays traceable and protected (R02, R04). Use the OneDrive/SharePoint offline sync on your encrypted company laptop instead.
The laptop is slow, so you switch off the antivirus for a while and install a free tool you found online to speed it up.
Disabling security functions and installing unapproved software is prohibited (R02). Talk to the Technical Lead instead.

Part 4

See something, say something

Nobody gets into trouble for reporting. People get into trouble when a lost laptop or a strange email is kept quiet for a week.

What counts as a security incident or event?

Report immediately

  • Laptop, phone or documents lost or stolen
  • You clicked a link or opened an attachment that turned out to be suspicious
  • Someone asks for your password or for data outside the normal channels
  • You can see project data you should not have access to
  • LabHarmony, AWS or SharePoint behave strangely, data looks changed or is missing
  • Personal data (CVs, contact details) was sent to the wrong person

How and to whom

  • Immediately to a Managing Director or the Technical Lead (IT/Security), via Teams or email, phone if urgent
  • Say what happened, when, and which data or systems are affected
  • Do not switch off or reset affected systems on your own; logs and system state are needed for the analysis
  • Also report near misses and weaknesses you notice, even if nothing happened

To take away

Five rules for every working day

  1. Your account is yours. Strong password, never shared, never reused.
  2. Lock it, hide it, encrypt it. Screen locked when you leave, laptop never unattended, privacy filter in public.
  3. Data stays in our cloud. SharePoint, Teams, AWS and LabHarmony, not USB sticks, private drives or AI chats.
  4. Access only for your project. Ask the project manager or a Managing Director, never a colleague's login.
  5. See something, say something. Lost device, strange email, wrong access: tell a Managing Director or the Technical Lead immediately.
